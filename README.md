# A Comparative Analysis of Variability in AI-Generated and Human-Written Text

An interactive research website presenting an independent computational linguistics study comparing the linguistic variability of AI-generated and human-written text.

The project combines a traditional research paper with interactive data visualizations and a live text analyser, allowing readers to explore the findings and compare their own writing against the study's statistical benchmarks.

---

## Overview

This research investigates whether AI-generated text exhibits lower linguistic variability than human-written text when responding to the same prompt.

The study analyzes:

- 172 human-written responses
- 150 AI-generated responses
- 5 large language models
- 5 linguistic metrics

The findings suggest that AI-generated text demonstrates substantially lower variance across multiple structural and lexical measures, indicating greater statistical consistency.

## Live Text Analyser

The built-in analyser allows users to paste any English text and instantly calculate:

- Word Count
- Sentence Count
- Unique Words
- Average Sentence Length
- Type–Token Ratio (TTR)

The application compares these values against the averages reported in the research and indicates whether the text more closely resembles the human or AI datasets.

## Research Summary

**Dataset**

- 172 Human Responses
- 150 AI Responses
- 5 Large Language Models

**Primary Findings**

- Human responses exhibited approximately **52× greater variance** in word count.
- Human sentence-length variability was approximately **20× greater**.
- Lexical diversity variability was approximately **17× greater**.

These findings suggest that AI-generated responses occupy a significantly narrower linguistic distribution than human-written responses under controlled prompt conditions.
